The lahar rivers

A lahar arriving at a gauge would look like a wall: the river rising many feet in minutes, far faster than any flood. These are the NOAA river gauges on the Puyallup, Carbon, White and Nisqually, upstream to downstream: the last 7 days, the official forecast (dashed) and the flood lines where NOAA sets them.

Why it matters

People in the hazard zones

90,000+

People live in Mount Rainier's lahar hazard zones, with over 50,000 employees at about 3,800 businesses (USGS, 2022).

Warning time

40 min – 3 h

Most communities, once a large lahar is detected. Inside the park, residential areas could be reached in about 5 minutes (USGS). More on warning time →

Lahars in the past

9+

Large lahars in the last 5,600 years. The Osceola Mudflow (2–3 km³) reached Puget Sound near Auburn; the Electron Mudflow, about 500 years ago, began as a landslide, with no eruption (USGS).
